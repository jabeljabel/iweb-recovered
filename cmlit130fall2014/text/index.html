Welcome to the World of Banned Books!

This course delves into the politics, ethics, and circulation of culture banned across several centuries and continents. For as long as books have been published, they have been classified as taboo, seized, and burned; their creators have been fined, jailed, tortured, and killed under many various political and religious regimes. Incorporating a range of systems of censorship from around the world, this course reveals differences in the modes of repression and the sometimes surprising connections between church and monarchy, fascism and democracy, capitalism and communism. What gets banned and why? How and when do bans occur? What effects do bans have on the circulation and legibility of texts? Who tries to offend the censors; who does so by mistake?
