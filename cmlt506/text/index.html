CMLT506

LITERATURE UNDER SURVEILLANCE

SPRING 2010

Literature Under Surveillance:  

Copyright, Censorship, and Artistic Production

CMLIT 506

Meeting Time: Thurs. 2:30-5:30

Room: 430 BURROWES

This course draws together two seemingly disparate aspects of the interactions between law and literature through the reality and metaphor of surveillance.

How does art push and exceed the legal restrictions imposed on it? Why are border-crossings and miscegenation continually deemed offensive? When are intellectual creations the property of an individual or a corporation?

This comparative seminar examines the stormy relationships between literature and the law.  Internationally, legal bans and copyright protections contravene their stated purposes when censorship fosters the literature it bans or when copyright becomes an obstacle to artistic expression.  Our consideration of past literary cases from around the globe will inform contemporary questions, including those surrounding derivative novels, digital reproduction, and password sharing
