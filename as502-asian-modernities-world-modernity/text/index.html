World Modernity

One modernity or many? One modernism or many? What makes these different questions?

MODERN MOMENT/S

Modernity means different things in different places at different times.  But does modernity also translate across cultures and over history?  This section examines the notion of a shared East Asian regional modernity in contrast to Western modernity.   Placing conceptions of alternative modernities and singular modernity in dialogue, the courselet focuses on the contested notions of Chinese, Japanese, and Korean modernities as exclusive of each other and of European modernity.   Readings will include works by Karatani Kojin, Harry Harootunian, Rey Chow, Rebecca Karl, and Gi Wook Shin.
