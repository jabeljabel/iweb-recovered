Close Reading Project

 

This is a group project that will result in a final individual writing assignment.

 

Tasks:

First, we must choose one canonical text of “world literature” that has been translated into the various languages we have represented in the seminar. We will decide on a text in our first meeting.

Second, you must obtain a copy of the text in your language of expertise.

Third, you must agree to a date to present your close reading.

Fourth, you must write one response to what you learned from the cumulative project of comparing the various close readings.

 

Some ideas for fun texts to do this with...

The Bible, Things Fall Apart, Around the World in Eighty Days, Gulliver’s Travels, Diary of a Madman, Don Quixote, Faust, Tristram Shandy, Othello, .....
