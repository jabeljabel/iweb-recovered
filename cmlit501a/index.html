This short seminar will examine the method of close reading as historical phenomenon. Through a brief look at the history of New Criticism, its rivals, and antecedents, this unit will examine Roland Barthes perceptive quip that "a little formalism turns one away from History, but that a lot brings one back to it." (Mythologies)  What does it mean to historically and closely read the practice of close reading? What will we discover about our methods? Our focus on the aesthetics of poetry, genre criticism, and media studies will define often overlooked relationships amongst close reading, deconstruction, historicism, and new historicism.
