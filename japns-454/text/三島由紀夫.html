「憂国」三島由紀夫

どちらが本気だろう？

１９６１

三島由紀夫

9/25/12

On Thursday.

Watch the film version of “Patriotism” starring Mishima.

Spend one hour or less only composing a short letter in Japanese written to the (now dead) author. Imagine he was alive. Present him your interpretation about the differences between the film and the short novel. Be sure to cite specific lines from the novel and scenes from the film which is streamed below thanks to YouTube.
