On the way towards or after NACH Connectivity...

Thursday, September 5, 2013

Starting with our first reading last....

Weber has a fascinating point here after Heidegger, a point that will get us to seek once again the technique within the technology of the bridge. But can we shift this question slightly to hopefully some useful insight by asking what is the techniquie of this new media (let’s say since web 2.0) technology of social media; the combination of web 2.0 and a smartphone has lead the mass media to talk of recent tech innovations being significant of connectivity. But what if connectivity is not the technique of technology but similar to what Weber wants to read Heidegger as claiming of the bridge, namely that all too often the bridge is simply seen as a connection of one place with another. But the technique hidden in the bridge is (Weber reminds us that Heidegger may have indeed intended) the way it brings forth revelations about place, the way the bridge in fact does not come after the place, but orders place, makes space place. So with our much touted 24-7 global connectivity, we need to ask what is the technique hidden by the fetish for the connection. Like the bridge that appear simply to connect two disparate places for which the technique is hidden by this simple and functional explanation, perhaps we need to dig beyond the connection of connectivity to see that when we ask what is connected the answer ‘us’ is not quite sufficient. For the us that is connect did not exist before the connection. Were we creating memes and videos of our cats en masse before the media?
