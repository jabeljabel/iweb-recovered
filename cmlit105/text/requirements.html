Description of Requirements

Attendance 1oPTS

The part of your grade that makes all others seem hard is attendance and participation. If you come to lectures and discussion sections, you will go a long way towards knowing and understanding the material.

Attendance and participation will be assessed through your clicking with your iClicker at lectures and discussion sections. Bring your iClicker with you to every class.

You are required to purchase an i>clicker remote for in-class participation.  i>clicker is a response system that allows you to respond to questions I pose during class, and you will be graded on that feedback and/or your in-class participation.  In order to receive this credit, you will need to register your i>clicker remote online within the first TWO WEEKS of class.  You must have come to class at least once and voted on at least one question in order to complete this registration properly. For more on i>clicker and instructions click here.

4 Theory quizzes 5pts each 20PTS

There will be brief multiple choice questions about theories of humor early in the class. This is to help you learn the vocabulary with which to discuss the literary humor we will read throughout the semester. Theories will be introduced in lectures and readings.

Group project and Presentation 10PTS

All students will be responsible for working with a group to develop a group project relating to a given week’s readings. See Group Project Guidelines for details.

Sign up for your group by the third week of class.

if you haven’t already Sign Up for Group project click here

Mid Term Exam 1 10pts

mid term Exam 2 10PTS

These will be multiple choice tests, taken in class to gauge whether (and how well) you have been keeping up with the readings and the lectures.

PAPERS (For all papers see the Paper Guidelines)

Paper 1 10pts

Take a stand. use one or more theories of humor to argue that your favorite Comedy film is not mean, vicious, or demeaning. This is a thought project to gauge how well you grasp and can make use of the theories of Humor to explain that which you have already experienced as funny.

2-3 pages

Paper 2 10pts

Choose one work of literature that we have read from a culture and time that is not your own. Research the context to the production of the story itself (information about the author or early readers), the time period in which it was produced, or the culture from which it came. answer the Following question: how do the details you have learned that exist outside of the words on the page help you understand those words? This is a research project designed to assess how well you can understand the humor of other times and places.

Final Paper 20PTS

compare two or more literary works from different cultures and contexts. What is similar about them? What is different? With history, language, and culture being so different across time and geography, What allows you to compare them? Theory of humor? Genre? or something else? Why is it important to compare? Answer the question: Is comparative literature a form of humor? Draw on your texts, outside research, and the theories of humor we have discussed to formulate your argument. 5-7 pages
