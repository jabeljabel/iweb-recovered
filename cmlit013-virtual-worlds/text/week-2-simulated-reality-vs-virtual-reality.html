Week 2 SImulated Reality Vs. Virtual Reality

When is realism reality? Where do we go to escape from our reality? How do we interact with that space for escapism? How do we control the space? When are we controlled by it? What are the genres that evoke automatic physical responses? Horror movies make us shift in our seats? Tear jerkers? Comedies make us laugh out loud? Porn makes us...? But do these genres make us do more?

Is the horror addict a potential axe murder? The porn addict a latent rapist? The comedy fan a fool? The tear jerker enthusiast unable to emote without fictional crutches?
