KEY PASSAGE?

“It must immediately be added that, in practice, structural analysis will also refer to real works: the best stepping-stone toward theory is that of precise, empirical knowledge. But such analysis will discover in each work what it has in common with other (study of genres, of periods, for example), or even with all other works (theory of literature)it would be unable to state the individual spedficity of each work. In practice, it is always a question of going continually back and forth, from abstract literary properties to individual works and vice versa. Poetics and description are in fact two complementary, activities.

On the other hand, to affirm the internal nature of this approach does not mean a denial of the relation between literature and other homogeneous series, such as philosophy or social life. It is rather a question of establishing a hierarchy; literature must be understood in its specificity, as literature, before we seek to determine its relation with anything else.”

PLOT

Why would Todorov be interested in Boccaccio? MAYBE Because as a set of a number of stories within a larger story, it performs as a mirror for the structure proposed by structuralism itself—a base underlying structure of literature (the frame story) and its manifestations in actual literary works (the stories within the story).

POTENTIAL PROBLEMS WITH THIS CHOICE OF EXAMPLE

Decameron is not a perfect mirror or corollary of the abstract structure and surface manifestation that structuralism entails. Decameron seems like a bunch of tales (is adopted from a bunch of tales) but is itself a single work. And thus potentially all Todorov has done is to show not a base structure at play in literature but one at play in this particular work.

What does T. mean when he says this method tells us much about “the universe of a book”?

“Such analysis seeks no longer to articulate a paraphrase, a rational resumé of the concrete work, but to propose a theory of the structure and operation of the literary discourse, to present a spectrum of literary possibilities, in such a manner that the existing works of literature appear as particular instances that have been realized.”

is odd in light of his original use of “resumé”:”The theme I propose to deal with is so vast that the few pages which follow will inevitably take the form of a resume.”

So though the theory proposed will not be a resumé, it will be presented in resumé format. Does the fact that form (style) and content (argument) do not agree in the essay matter? Would they matter if this were not an essay but literature?

-- Critic T. identifies 2 attitudes toward literature

-- sides with one of them (the subordinate one)

--- Critic T. identifies 2 methods of reading

-- sides with one of them (the dominant one)

--Critic T. tells us what is wrong with the method as typically employed

--Critic T. shows how the 2 attitudes and 2 methods are related in opposite way as typically conceived

--Critic T. chooses old critic to lampoon

--Critic T. employs traditional example for radical reading

--THE END
