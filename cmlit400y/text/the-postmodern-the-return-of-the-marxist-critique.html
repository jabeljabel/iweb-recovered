What would this mean for theory?

Is there a premodern, modern, and postmodern theory? Pre-realism, realism, post-realism kind of theory? Pre, high, post theory moments? If so, Jameson’s seems on the cusp. A throwback to the modern or high theory that believed in something, that called itself by a name, prepastiche and yet not straight marxism. What is the formalism doing there? Why Greimas? Is this a product of its time? Is the existence of a pastiche-like postmodern moment in which theories are taught ensemble out of Norton a post-theory moment?
